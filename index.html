About Us

MK Dance Studio Lampung is a place where passion and talent grow together. Located in Bandar Lampung, we provide professional dance training for kids, teens, and adults in a fun, supportive, and inspiring environment. At MK Dance Studio, students can learn a variety of popular dance styles including K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance. Our programs are designed to help students build confidence, improve coordination, and develop strong performance skills both on and off the stage. As one of the growing dance studios in Lampung, we focus not only on technique but also on creativity, expression, and teamwork. Our experienced instructors guide each student step by step, from beginner level to advanced choreography, ensuring every dancer can reach their full potential. We believe that dance is more than just movement — it is a way to express emotions, build discipline, and create unforgettable experiences. That is why MK Dance Studio has become a trusted choice for dance classes in Bandar Lampung, especially for parents looking for a positive and professional environment for their children. Join MK Dance Studio Lampung today and start your journey in dance with us. Whether you are looking for a dance class for kids, teens, or adults, we are here to help you grow, perform, and shine on stage.

Our Classes

MK 1

is designed for children aged 3-5 years. This class introduces basic movements in a fun and playful environment, helping kids develop coordination, rhythm, and confidence through K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance

MK 2

is for children aged 6-7 years. Students begin to learn more structured dance techniques while exploring different styles such as K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance in an engaging and supportive environment.

MK 3

is for children aged 8-9 years. This level focuses on improving coordination, musicality, and performance skills, with a variety of dance styles including K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance.

MK 4

is designed for children aged 10-12 years. Students develop stronger technique, expression, and stage presence while learning diverse dance styles such as K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance.

MK Teens

is for students aged 13 years and above. This class focuses on building confidence, performance quality, and advanced choreography through a mix of K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance styles.

MK Adults

is designed for adults of all levels, from beginners to experienced dancers. Classes focus on fitness, self-expression, and enjoyment through dynamic styles such as K-pop, Hip-hop, and Modern Dance..
